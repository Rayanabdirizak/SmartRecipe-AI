🍕 Italian

Cheesy Pizza

⭐ 4.9 ⏱️ 25 min 🍽️ 4 Servings

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough
  • Mozzarella Cheese
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Fresh Basil
  • Olive Oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Spread tomato sauce on the dough.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese.
  4. Bake for 20–25 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with fresh basil.
