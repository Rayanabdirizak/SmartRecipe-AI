Italian

Cheesy Pizza

Crispy homemade pizza topped with mozzarella, tomato sauce, herbs and fresh vegetables.

⭐ 4.9
⏱ 25 Minutes
👨‍🍳 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

  • ✔ 2 Cups Flour
  • ✔ Tomato Sauce
  • ✔ Mozzarella Cheese
  • ✔ Oregano
  • ✔ Olive Oil
  • ✔ Fresh Basil
  • ✔ Black Pepper
  • ✔ Salt

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Prepare the pizza dough and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Spread tomato sauce evenly on the dough.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and vegetables.
  4. Sprinkle oregano and basil.
  5. Bake at 220°C for about 20–25 minutes.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy your delicious homemade pizza.
